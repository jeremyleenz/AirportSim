# Airport Ops expansion roadmap

Start with a small airport and give players time to learn its operations. Airport improvements should make a comparable shift easier to run. Players can take on more traffic when they’re ready.

## 1. Start with a smaller airport

Start with two gates, one baggage carousel, two check-in counters, and two security lanes. Staff one counter and one lane at opening. The second of each gives players a way to respond when queues build.

Start the first day with one overnight aircraft and a free gate for the first inbound. Stagger early flights so players can handle an arrival and a departure before more turnarounds overlap. Two gates let players choose a stand and recover from a late departure. A well-run first shift should be manageable with the starting facilities.

## 2. Carry the treasury across shifts

Carry the airport’s balance into the next day so operating profit can fund improvements. Use the shift report to show where capacity ran short. For baggage claim, distinguish arrivals that waited while every belt was occupied from arrivals that waited while a belt was available but unassigned.

## 3. Offer the first two improvements

Let players buy a second carousel or more shared seating between shifts. Use fixed build spots for both. Set prices so a decent first shift makes either purchase affordable.

- **Second carousel** lets two arrivals use baggage claim at once. Players still choose which carousel each arrival uses.
- **Shared seating** gives more waiting passengers somewhere to sit. Show the difference in the terminal during the next shift.

Give the next shift a comparable workload so players can tell whether their purchase helped. The first playable milestone is finishing a shift, using the report to choose an improvement, and seeing its effect the next day.

## 4. Add more operational expansion

Offer a third gate, additional check-in counters, and additional security lanes. Use observed bottlenecks to decide which to develop first. Show players when flights held because all gates were occupied or queues persisted with every available counter or lane staffed.

## 5. Introduce optional airline contracts

Start with one base airline and offer a small optional contract between shifts. Show the proposed arrival and departure times, passenger and baggage volumes, and expected revenue. A contract in a quieter window may fit the current airport. A contract during a busy arrival bank may justify building capacity first.

Purchases increase capacity. Signing a contract adds flights to the timetable. Keeping those decisions independent gives players control over the difficulty as their airport grows.

## 6. Add richer amenities

- **Restaurants** once passenger spending can affect airport revenue.
- **Toilets** once passenger needs can affect their experience.
- **Travelators** once walking distances can affect whether passengers reach their gates on time.
